Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you plan your visit.

What is Tailor Expo?

Tailor Expo is an exhibition concept focused on tailoring, sewing technology, textiles, embroidery, fashion and garment manufacturing.

When is the event?

The event dates are December 13–16. The year has not yet been provided, so please confirm it with the organizers.

What are the timings?

The stated timings are 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.

Where will Tailor Expo take place?

Raikar Exhibition Ground, Hubli, Karnataka. Please confirm the exact location pin before travelling.

Who can attend?

Fashion designers, tailors, sewing machine dealers, embroidery professionals, garment manufacturers and general visitors are the intended audiences.

Is entry free? Do I need to register?

Entry fees and final registration requirements have not been confirmed. Please check with the organizers for the latest information.

How can my business participate as an exhibitor?

Use the enquiry form below to express interest. The organizers can then share confirmed stall availability, pricing and participation details.
